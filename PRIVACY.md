# Privacy Policy

**xkcd-Adopted** is a free, open-source xkcd comic reader for iOS, maintained by Cedar Syntax LLC.

## Data Collection

xkcd-Adopted does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

- No analytics or tracking of any kind
- No advertising or ad networks
- No user accounts or authentication
- No crash reporting services
- No cookies or local identifiers sent to third parties

## Network Activity

The app connects only to **xkcd.com** and its image CDN (**imgs.xkcd.com**) to fetch comic content. No user data is transmitted in these requests beyond what is standard in any HTTPS connection (your IP address, as seen by xkcd.com's servers).

If you use the "Explain" feature, the app opens **explainxkcd.com** in a browser view. This is a user-initiated navigation and is subject to explainxkcd.com's own privacy practices.

## Data Storage

Comic metadata and images are cached locally on your device using Realm and SDWebImage. This data never leaves your device.

## Children's Privacy

xkcd-Adopted does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, updates will be posted here and in the app's GitHub repository.

## Contact

Questions or concerns? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ranvel/xkcd-Adopted/issues) or reach out via the App Store support link.
